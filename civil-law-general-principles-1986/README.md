# General Principles of the Civil Law of the PRC (Uyghur) — 1986

Uyghur-language text of the *General Principles of the Civil Law of the People's
Republic of China* (جۇڭخۇا خەلق جۇمھۇرىيىتى ھەق-تەلەپ قانۇنىنىڭ ئومۇمىي قائىدىسى).

Adopted on 12 April 1986 at the 4th session of the 6th National People's Congress
and promulgated the same day by Presidential Decree No. 37; in force from
1 January 1987.

## Contents

- `heq-telep-qanuni-1986-ug.md` — full text in 9 chapters (156 articles).

## Provenance

The Uyghur text was taken from uyghur-archive.com and has not been independently
verified against an official PRC release. It is retained here as the best available
Uyghur-language copy; a source closer to an official publication should supersede
it if one becomes available.
