# PRC Government Work Reports (in Uyghur) Regarding Occupied East Turkistan— 2016–2025

Uyghur-language texts of the annual Government Work Report (ھۆكۈمەت خىزمىتىدىن دوكلاتى)
delivered to the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region People's Congress, covering the
work years 2016 through 2025.

Each report was published by the autonomous region's official Uyghur-language portal
(`uygur.xinjiang.gov.cn`) and originally carried in *Xinjiang Gëziti* (شىنجاڭ گېزىتى).
The source permalink and publication date are recorded in the header of each report
within the document.

## Notes on this text

- Assembled from the official Uyghur-language releases linked above.
- Dash characters (`-`, en-dash, minus) that appeared inconsistently in the source
  have been normalized to a plain hyphen with no surrounding spaces in compound words
  and numeric suffixes (e.g. `14-نۆۋەتلىك`, `خەۋپ-خەتەر`).
- No wording has been altered; changes are limited to whitespace, dash normalization,
  and Markdown structure.
